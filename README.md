 Lost & Found University System

A centralized platform where students, staff, and visitors can report, search for, and recover lost items across university buildings.

Author: Rahaf Jasem Joudeh (ID: 202312280) Course: Special Topics in Computer Science — Al-Zaytoonah University of Jordan

Problem

Lost items on campus (ID cards, electronics, keys, bags) are currently handled through word-of-mouth, social media posts, and notice boards. Recovery is slow and many items go unclaimed.

Features
Report lost or found items with description, photo, and location
Search and filter by keyword, category, date, and location
Automatic matching of lost and found reports
Email/app notifications when a match appears
Claim verification and admin approval before handover
Admin dashboard to manage reports
Out of Scope

Courier delivery, compensation or insurance claims, university-asset tracking, and external lost & found integrations.

Key Requirements
Mobile-friendly, simple interface
Search results in under 2 seconds for 95% of queries
Role-based access with encrypted personal data
99% uptime during the academic term
Main Risk

Fraudulent claims, mitigated with verification questions and admin approval before handover.

Status

Planning and requirements phase.
